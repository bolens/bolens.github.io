# Scene behavior contract
Explicit scene time overrides appearance. Outdoor human/dog occupancy and tent
occupancy are complements. The tent camera stays inside with the woman at night.
Tree hideouts share their cover's motion plane. New glyphs publish data-regions
and consume existing terrain light/weather/detail variables.

Saucer endpoint hiding uses physical paint order behind retained mountains, not a
fade-to-zero trick. A reduced-motion override leaves the craft partly exposed.
New elements do not expand the renderer's live target list.
